Welcome to SprayCast 🌦

Start on the ⚙ Setup tab and add your 📍 location (type your town — that's the whole setup). Then this Planner shows how many sprayable hours each day offers under your active ⚙ profile, the 📌 button up top commits sprays, and the ⏰ Watches tab alerts you when the right window shows up. Your data stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
⏰ Spray watches
A watch is a spray you're waiting to make: it checks every forecast day at its location(s) and lights up when a long-enough window appears — penciled onto the Planner grid as a dashed ⏰ outline. One-time watches retire when you commit them; recurring ones keep watching.
📌 Committed sprays
⚠ on a spray means the forecast has SHIFTED since you committed it and some of its hours are now outside your limits — with 🔔 Alerts on, SprayCast chimes when that happens.

📤 Committed sprays → a shared Google Sheet
Give the whole crew (or the boss) a live list of what's planned without anyone opening SprayCast: commits — and later done/cancel updates — are written to a Google Sheet you own. One-time setup, about 3 minutes:
  1. Make a Google Form with short-answer questions: Date, Start, End, Spray, Location, Notes, Status (name them however you like — Date/Time question types work too). Leave every question NOT-required, set Settings → Responses → Collect email addresses: OFF — and then click Publish (top right; a form that was never published rejects every row).
  2. In the form's Responses tab click the Sheets icon — that spreadsheet is your shared list (share/publish it like any sheet).
  3. In the form: ⋮ menu → Get pre-filled link → type the words date, start, end, spray, location, notes, status into their matching boxes → Get link → copy it → paste below.
Sends are one-way and invisible (Google doesn't let the page read the result) — the 🧪 test row confirms the pipe once. The connection stays in this browser and rides your backup/share file, so a coworker who imports it writes to the same sheet.
💾 Backup & sharing
Your data lives only in this browser. The backup file is also how you share your setup with a coworker — they import it on their computer.
ℹ About SprayCast

Privacy, plainly: there is no account, no install, and nothing you enter is uploaded anywhere — unless you connect the optional 📤 Google-Sheet bridge, in which case committed sprays (and nothing else) are written to the Google Form/Sheet you set up. Your locations, profiles, watches and plans are stored only in this browser on this computer. The internet requests are to Open-Meteo (a free public weather service) for the forecast and town search, — only when you open the 🧭 wind map — Esri's public satellite imagery tiles, and — only when connected — your own Google form.

A forecast is a forecast: SprayCast is planning help, not a decision-maker. The product label always wins, and check live conditions before you pull out of the yard.

⚙ Condition profiles

A profile is what "sprayable" means to you: wind, gusts, rain chance, temperature — and which hours you're willing to spray, per weekday (night sprayers and long Sundays welcome).
Wind carries drift the opposite way — check the directions that are safe for what sits downwind. All unchecked = any direction.
Uncheck a day to never plan sprays on it.
