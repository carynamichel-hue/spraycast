# SprayCast

Spray window planner — helps you plan spraying around the weather, judged on the
forecast at your farm against your own limits for wind, gusts, rain chance and
temperature, with the drift direction for every hour.

**Use it here: https://carynamichel-hue.github.io/spraycast/**

Free, no accounts, nothing to install. Add the town your farm is in and it
starts telling you which hours work. Set watches for the conditions you're
waiting on, schedule sprays and export them to your calendar, and — optionally —
bring in your own station readings (LI-COR, a NEWA station, or a CSV your
station exported) or the National Weather Service forecast as a second opinion.

This repository holds the **built site**. There is no server and no account —
your data stays in your browser, and the ℹ About panel in ⚙ Setup lists exactly
what leaves it and what never does.
